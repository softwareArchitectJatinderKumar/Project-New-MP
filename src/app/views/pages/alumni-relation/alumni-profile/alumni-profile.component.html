Process to create alumni profile –

  1. Open the LPU Alumni website.
  2. Click "Login" and continue with Google using your personal email ID.
  3. Fill in your basic information.
  4. Go to your profile and copy the profile link.
  5. For step-by-step guidelines on creating an alumni profile, please refer to the official Click Here .

Benefits of Registering on Alumni Website

  • Networking: Expand your professional network by engaging with accomplished alumni from diverse fields and industries using the alumni directory.
  • Mentorship: Engage with seniors to navigate your academic and professional paths by selecting alumni mentor of your choice.
  • Hiring Opportunities: Access various jobs and lateral hiring opportunities provided by the alumni.
  • Stay Informed: Access the latest news about your alma mater, upcoming events, reunions, and awards.
  • Showcase Achievements: Share your accomplishments and milestones with fellow alumni, inspiring others with your journey, and check out stories of others.
  • Alumni Chapter Leaders: Avail a chance to become a local chapter core committee member and lead alumni chapters in your respective location.
  • Request Service: Apply for certificates, transcripts, letters of recommendation, document verification, gate pass, and more easily in one click.
